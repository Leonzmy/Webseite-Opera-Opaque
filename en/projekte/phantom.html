Phantom is a music theatre evening about the voice in the age of its digital manipulability. Almost all singing and speaking voices are AI-generated; only a children's choir is real. The staging combines laboratory, song recital, and drag show, features live generation from audience voices, and asks what happens to identity once we can no longer trust the voice.

The production was awarded the City of Munich's "Junge Kunst / Neue Medien" (Young Art / New Media) grant.
